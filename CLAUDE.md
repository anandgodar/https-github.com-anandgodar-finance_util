## AdSense fix (Nov 2026 — "Low value content", rejected 4 Nov)
Root cause: 204 state pages across four tiers (`/freelance-profit-hub/`, `/early-retirement-fire-planner/`, `/mortgage-calculator/`, `/quarterly-tax-calculator/` × 51) were **98% identical boilerplate** with the state name and a few unsourced figures swapped — GSC 28d to 16 Nov: ~250 impressions, 0 clicks across all 204 (site total: 2 clicks / 17,229 impressions). Plus five tool pages at 111–246 body words with ads serving (the tools are lazy client components, so their text never reached the static HTML).

What stands now:
- The four tiers are **not built**; templates preserved in `retired-templates/` (see its README for the restore bar). Their URLs 301 via vercel.json wildcards to each national tool. `/salary-tax-estimator/{state}` × 51 **stays** — sourced per-state tax tables (`lib/state-tax-2026`, gated by `check-state-tax.mjs`), ~71% unique.
- `market-intelligence-pulse` (38-word AI-generated shell), `gst-tax-calculator` (India GST on a US-tax site) and `cost-of-living-calculator` (widget on unsourced COL indexes) are retired the same way; each 301s to its blog guide or home.
- `components/ToolProse.tsx` server-renders methodology prose on the DRIP calculator, options visualizer and investment academy, so their words are in the HTML regardless of hydration. Rule: describe only what the tool computes; no invented statistics.
- `scripts/check-content.mjs` fails the build on: any page under 300 body words outside its small EXEMPT list (**noindex does not exempt — AdSense reviews the live site**), a retired URL being built, any built page linking a redirected URL, a redirect whose target is missing, and a sitemap listing an unbuilt/noindex page. Fault-injected 17 Nov: each fires.
- `lib/state-configs.ts` figures (tax rates, home prices, COL indexes) are **hand-typed and unsourced** — never present them as data; they must not return to rendered pages without sources.
- Review round (17 Nov): the gate's link rule missed JSON-LD `url` fields (href= only) — rule 3b added, fault-injected. The retirement had also left the retired tools advertised in JS-navigation UI the href scan can't see: AppShell's **mobile drawer** (GST + Market Pulse on 44 pages), the homepage hero "View Market Pulse" CTA, SiteShell's 🤖 nav icon, the /sitemap card, FAQ links, and the three retired-tool blog guides' own "Open …" CTAs (which 301-looped back to the guide). All retargeted or removed. ToolProse corrected against the code: the DRIP model is **annual** and the no-reinvest path **drops** dividends (no side-cash balance); the options chart is **already ×100 per contract**, has no covered-call preset (long call / spreads / iron condor / iron butterfly / straddle / strangle, 1–4 legs, more addable), and its IV line + Greeks panel are crude illustrations, said so; the academy prose no longer describes sequenced "guides" and prospectus sourcing that don't exist. When retiring a tool, grep for its ToolType across `components/` — nav lives in more than `Sidebar.tsx`.
